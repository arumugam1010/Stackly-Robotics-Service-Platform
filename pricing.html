PRICING ASSISTANCE

Pricing FAQs

Q: Are there setup fees?

No setup fees. You only pay the per-robot-unit license fee when active.

Q: Can we upgrade plans?

Yes, you can upgrade from Standard to Enterprise billing at any time.

Q: Do you offer a free trial?

We offer a 14-day fully featured sandbox environment to test our API endpoints.

Q: Are hardware costs included?

No, our pricing only covers the Stackly cloud management software and middleware.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, bank transfers, and standard purchase orders.

Q: Can we self-host the platform?

Yes, local on-premise container deployment is available on our Enterprise plan.

Q: Is there a discount for annual billing?

Yes, paying annually grants a 15% discount across all standard and popular plans.
